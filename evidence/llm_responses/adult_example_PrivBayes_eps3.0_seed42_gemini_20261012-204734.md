## Limitations and caveats

- **Synthetic Distortion & Inversion**: Small cell counts and DP noise produce anomalies. These counter-intuitive non-monotonic patterns are likely noise artifacts exacerbated by small sample sizes (each represents $\le 3\%$ of rows).

This eliminates fine-grained continuous correlations (explaining Pearson $r \approx 0$). Furthermore, capital gain/loss quartiles collapsed into single degenerate bins, preventing continuous quantile analysis.
- **Fairness and Subgroup Representation**: Smaller demographic groups (e.g., `Amer-Indian-Eskimo` at 3.0%, `Married-spouse-absent` at 2.9%) are prone to heavy relative DP noise and suppression. For instance, `Married-spouse-absent` shows an estimated 0.0% positive income rate, which likely reflects noise truncation or suppression rather than true ground truth.
- **Privacy Scope**: The aggregate summary is a post-processed release from the synthetic table; neither this summary nor the analysis adds any differential privacy guarantees beyond the original PrivBayes mechanism ($\epsilon = 3.0, \delta = 0$). Quality context metrics were evaluated against real data and do not carry DP guarantees.
