## Limitations and caveats

- **DP noise and small-cell instability:** Differential privacy injects noise to protect individual privacy. Small sub-populations are particularly vulnerable to distortion. For instance, the counter-intuitive $>50\text{K}$ rate for `12th` grade (**43.3%** on a 1.3% row share) and `Assoc-voc` surpassing `Masters` are likely noise artifacts rather than authentic social trends. Similarly, rates of **0.0%** for `Unmarried`, `Other-relative`, and `Married-spouse-absent` reflect small sample or thresholding effects.
- **Discretisation and reconstruction artifacts:** Numerical features were generated over binned representations and reconstructed by uniform sampling. As a result, capital gains and losses show degenerate quartile bins (spanning 100% of rows), obscuring finer non-zero dynamics, and true continuous correlations are severely attenuated.
- **Algorithmic constraints:** The round cap (60 iterations) limits the mechanism’s ability to capture high-order cross-tabulations, corroborated by the 3-way marginal error of **0.189**.
- **Fairness and representation risks:** Demographic distributions are heavily skewed (e.g., White represents **85.5%**, US native country represents **89.0%**, Amer-Indian-Eskimo represents **1.0%**). Subgroup analyses on small groups carry high statistical noise and risk generating unreliable fairness conclusions.
- **Information boundaries:** The summary does not provide multivariate conditional distributions (e.g., income stratified by sex and occupation simultaneously), precluding causal claims. Neither this analysis nor the LLM provides privacy guarantees beyond the generator's stated $(\epsilon, \delta)$ mechanism.
